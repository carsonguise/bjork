General Information

Björk was born on November 21st, 1965 in Reykjavik, Iceland to a musical household. Björk’s full name is Björk Guðmundsdóttir, which means “birch-tree” in Icelandic (pronounced Beyerk). She began her career when she released her first album when she was only 11 years old that contained traditional Icelandic folk songs. She has many attributes that makes her a double threat; having great success in music and winning awards for her acting.

Her three-octave vocal range and eccentric persona have carried her to her fame. She embarked on her solo career with her album “Debut” which strayed away from the harder-edged sound of the Sugarcubes (a band from her childhood she created), but included music ranging from techno-pop to jazz. Her four decade career has expanded on electronic, prop, experimental, trip hop, classical, and avant-garde music. She has many career successes, like performing at the Athens, Greece Summer Olympics of 2004 where she sang “Oceania” off of her album Medúlla.
