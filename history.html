In her teen years she sang in anarchist punk banks, like KUKL, with members including Einar Örn, Siggi Baldursson and Einar Melax. Before the Sugarcubes, she was involved with other punk and post-punk bands such as Spit and Snot, Exodus, Tappi Tikarrass, and KUKL. She is credited as one of the founding members of the band Sugarcubes (1986) with members Magga Örnólfsdóttir, Bragi Ólafsson and guitarist Thór Eldon. The Sugarcubes first full-length debut was “Life’s Too Good” in 1988 that earned them a following in the US and UK, and their final album was in 1992 called “It’s It” which was a collection of dance remixes of their earlier material, due to Björk's obsession with the genre. Once the band disbanded, she then entered into her solo career.

In terms of romantic involvements throughout her career, on June 8th, 1986 Björk and Thór Eldon (members of the Sugarcubes band) married and had a son, Síndri Eldon Thórsson, but then later divorced in 1987. After her divorce, she was romantically involved with Goldie and Tricky (of Massive Attack) and Matthew Barney (artist of the Cremaster project) to which she met during promoting Dancer in the Dark and started dating and are still together. They have a daughter, Isadora Bjarkardottir Barney, and in October 3rd, 2002 they began working on the film Drawing Restraint 9 in 2005 and still live in NYC together.

She has acting success as well as musical success. In 2000 appeared in the film Dancer in the Dark, to which she created the score for, and won best actress at the Cannes Film Festival and earned herself a Golden Globe nomination for it as well.

Her craft is known for being out of the ordinary but in an interesting and captivating way. One interesting fact about her song “Cover Me” from her album “Post” is that it was recorded in a bat-infested cave in Nassau, the Bahamas. Likewise, she has made her fashion statement wearing extraordinary costumes by Alexander McQueen to build her intriguing facets of her career. Her statement in the music world dissolves binary oppositions, many of times mixing nature and technology in her music in innovative ways that at first caused her to have the niche type of music her fans craved.
