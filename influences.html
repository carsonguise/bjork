There are many artists, such as Kate Bush, who Björk has stated she has been influenced by. Kate Bush, known as “The Queen of alternative pop”, Björk said that the “matriarch electronic music” she used in her album “Debut” combined the nineties British electronic music with her inspiration from Kate Bush. Her favorite album of Kate Bush’s is “Dreaming.”

On the other hand, other artists such as Radiohead, FKA Twigs, Evanescence, and many more have stated that Björk has influenced their music. Radiohead has stated that she has changed their musical approach on their album Kid A, changing their guitar driven sound to synthesizers, ambient soundscapes, and less traditional instruments and rhythm. They also have said that “Homogenic” has changed everything for them. FKA Twigs is known for mixing different genres from Hip Hop to Electronica to Avant-Garde to Soul, driven by Björk’s album “Homogenic”. The dance and hip hops represent her legacy and can be heard on Twigs’ albums Magdalene and Caprisongs. FKA Twigs stage presence and performance antics stand out just like Björks. Evanescence's Amy Lee has said that she loved Björk’s live performances of Cornucopia and she is a big inspiration to her. In 2001 she said that “Vespertine” was her favorite Björk album, connecting to the lyrics about letting go of the past and embracing acceptance, themes she incorporates into her own songs. She is also very found of “Debut” and “Post.”
